Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
They start to stretch and cover the entire screen, which starts to lose the effectiveness of a BVHtree.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
The bounding boxes now jump from pair to pair, creating boxes with the least area. This leaves the tree looking more readable,
and more effiencient, as less large squares have to be drawn.

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
I did not notice any circles with their sides cut off. I was told this happens if you update the circles before updating
the bounding boxes, but I never noticed it.
